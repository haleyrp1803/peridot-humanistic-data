# Home H2c — Editorial stock asset provenance

Original stock assets are supplied and reported as licensed by the project owner. Verify that the particular Adobe Stock license permits public website display and storage in the project's public Git repository before publishing or committing source images. This package contains **derived assets only**, not the original stock masters. Adobe Stock license terms and any necessary attribution remain the owner's responsibility to verify.

- `home-stock-torn-hero-mask.png` is a derived grayscale alpha mask based on the tear profile of `AdobeStock_572861950.png`. No original stock artwork is directly displayed; it controls transparency of a CSS-generated paper layer.
- `home-stock-feature-parchment.webp` is a cropped and optimized derivative of `AdobeStock_2224341756.png`, retaining transparency and paper texture.
- `home-stock-paper-texture.webp` is a resized and optimized derivative of `AdobeStock_126953722.jpeg`.

Historic works from H2a remain unchanged: `rosselli-florence.webp`, `benincasa-portolan-detail.webp` and their existing `ASSET_CREDITS.md`.

No decorative routes or compass ornaments are added. H2b's `home-route-decal.svg` may remain on disk but is no longer referenced by this Home layout. It can be removed in a later explicit cleanup pass.
